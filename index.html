Nanacon · Hamster Waterslide Tycoon

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 22, 2026

This policy explains how Nanacon and the services in Hamster Waterslide Tycoon for Android handle information when you play the game, see an ad, or use the in-game shop.

Gameplay and device storage

You can start playing as a guest. The game stores park progress, settings, and purchase entitlements on your device so you can resume play and restore eligible items. Clearing the app's storage removes that local progress.

Ads and diagnostics

We use Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads to deliver and measure ads, including optional ads that grant in-game rewards. The advertising services may collect and share approximate location inferred from network information, device and advertising identifiers, SDK identifiers, ad interactions, and diagnostics. They use this information to serve and measure ads, operate their services, and detect fraud. The Unity SDKs transmit the data they collect using encryption in transit.

Advertising preferences and identifiers can be managed in Android's privacy settings. Availability and names of controls depend on the Android version and region. See Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy for information about Unity's processing and privacy choices.

Optional purchases

Google Play Billing processes payments for optional in-game items. Unity In-App Purchasing handles purchase and entitlement information so the game can deliver items and restore eligible purchases. Unity's purchase services may also process SDK identifiers and performance information to operate and diagnose purchases. Google handles payment-method details under Google's Privacy Policy.

Sharing and retention

Unity and its advertising partners process advertising and diagnostic information. Google and Unity process purchase information for billing and entitlement purposes. Service providers apply their own retention periods. Park progress and settings remain on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the game, subject to any device backup settings.

Audience

The game is intended for players age 13 and older. A parent or guardian may contact us with any concern about a child's information.

Privacy requests and contact

To ask about information associated with the game or request access, correction, or deletion, contact Nanacon at nanaconcto@gmail.com. Include the game name so we can direct your request. For information handled directly by Unity or Google, you can also use the privacy request channels linked in their policies.

We will update the date above when we make changes to this policy.
